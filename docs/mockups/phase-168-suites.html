Phase 168 — Rooms inside rooms

A room can have rooms inside it, one level deep: floor, room, room inside, spot. The owner's LT2 below. Plan: docs/phase-168-suites-plan.md. Owners see English or Bahasa Indonesia; staff screens are Bahasa Indonesia only.

1. Chores → Rooms, "Vacuum" picked

Rooms inside sit under their room, always open, with a thin rule on the left. Every room counts once, so LT2 reads "Vacuum in 5 of 9" and the walk-in closet shows it has no vacuum yet (D4). Bathrooms are quiet, not red.

3. Chore form: the room picker

Rooms inside are indented under their room. When adding, "Whole Master Bedroom" ticks the bedroom and each room inside, one chore each (D5). Search matches the whole path, so "kamar mandi" finds all three bathrooms with their bedroom.
