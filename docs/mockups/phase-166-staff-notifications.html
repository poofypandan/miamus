2. The card on an iPhone in Safari, and on a blocked phone

On iPhone, push only works from the Home Screen (iOS 16.4+), so the card shows the steps instead. The Home Screen app has its own storage, so staff sign in once more with the house code and PIN. When the phone has blocked notifications, the card says where to allow them and shows again after 30 days. The second card is for a browser whose push service isn't on the allow-list (for example some HeyTap/Realme browsers).

3. On the lock screen: a personal phone and a shared phone

Same events, different text. A personal phone shows the details. A shared phone (two or more people with notifications on, or two or more PIN sign-ins in 30 days) says only whose it is. Task pushes replace each other for the day, so the lock screen keeps one per person. A shared phone never shows task names, leave dates or repair details.

One row at the bottom of the Lapor sheet (D7). It opens a sheet with one switch for the person on duty, the four things that are sent, the quiet hours and a test. There are no per-event options. "Matikan di HP ini (untuk semua)" removes the subscription for everyone on the phone, after a confirm step.

6. Owner: Household → Notifications, the staff phones switch (EN / ID)

One household switch (D8), on by default, under the owner's own "Notifications on this phone" card from #50. Owners can't turn notifications on for staff, because only the phone can allow them.

7. Owner: who has them on, and the toast after saving (EN / ID)

The Staff list shows each person's status, and Linked staff phones shows whose notifications each phone carries. After saving a chore for today, the toast says the person will get a notification, so the owner doesn't need to send a WhatsApp as well. With the switch off or in quiet hours, it says only "Saved." (The toast really appears in Chores after saving. It is drawn here to save space.)
