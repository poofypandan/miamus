Phase 174: repairs from the staff side, "Ada tukang datang"

Mockups for docs/phase-174-repairs-walkin-plan.md. Staff screens: Bahasa Indonesia only. Owner screens: English left, Bahasa Indonesia right. Icons are the app's lucide line icons; no emoji. Names, rooms and times are examples.

1. Staff: the Perbaikan pill (Bahasa Indonesia only)

A third pill, shown only when Repairs is on. The big button first; then what's being worked on (tinted), today's scheduled visits, what's still broken, and what was fixed this week. Lapor kerusakan stays for problems with no tukang yet.

2. Staff: Ada tukang datang (Bahasa Indonesia only)

One sheet, no wizard. Left: an open repair picked; its last tukang is already chosen; the before photo taken. 7 taps from the pill (6 with no name). Right: Baru, a few words and a room chip. Name, room and spot are optional; "what" and the photo are the only required parts.

3. Staff: Selesai, and the finished card (Bahasa Indonesia only)

Left: from the card's Selesai: the after photo (live camera, required), an optional chip and note. 4 to 5 taps. Right: the card in Sudah diperbaiki, before and after side by side, with the times.

4. Owners hear about it: arrival and finish pushes

Two pushes, each switchable per owner in Household → Notifications, none between 21.00 and 06.00. The finish push replaces the arrival push. Android shows the after photo; iPhone shows the text.

5. Owners: the repair, before and after

Chores → Repairs → the repair. The walk-in sits in the timeline with who, time in and out, who logged it, both photos, the chip and the note. The existing buttons close it.

6. Owners: send the list to the developer

Chores → Repairs → Send to the developer. All open problems ticked; untick what isn't theirs. Share on WhatsApp (text and photos through the phone's share sheet) or Save as PDF. Owners only; no public link.

7. Staff: supervising the repair, "Mengawasi perbaikan" (Bahasa Indonesia only)

Left: Ari's Tugas Hari Ini while she supervises the AC repair. The strip shows when it started and how long it has run, with Selesai. Her tasks due meanwhile carry a quiet "Mengawasi perbaikan" label instead of turning late: nothing to tap. Right: for an edge case auto-explain leaves alone (here a task with no time), the Tidak bisa dikerjakan sheet offers "Mengawasi perbaikan" on a day she supervised, linked to the visit, with no note to type. For a task whose time falls inside her stretch it is already chosen.

8. Owners: the evening summary explains missed tasks

139's evening push, re-created: tasks missed while Ari supervised are still "Not done", with the stretch in brackets once, not per task.

9. Owners: staff progress on Today and the summary sheet

Top: the queued staff progress card, one row per staff member, reading the active supervision from this phase. Below: the summary sheet groups the explained tasks under Ari's stretch; a tap opens the repair.

Not shown: Household → Notifications gains two rows, "When a repair person arrives" and "When a repair is finished" (Saat tukang datang · Saat perbaikan selesai), both on by default. Today gains a quiet line while someone is working.
