B1: first-run checklist and calmer staff Today

Audit batch B1 (docs/product-audit-2026-11-09.md, items 3 and 5). Client only: no migration, no new data, no privacy change. Phone-width mockups; the build matches these.

Owner screens in English (left) and Bahasa Indonesia (right). Staff screens are Bahasa Indonesia only: before (left) and after (right).

When the checklist shows, and when it goes

  • Who sees it: owners and family admins, on Today, Day view only. Never on staff phones.
  • Each step ticks itself from real data: the home exists; someone on the Staff list; a staff phone linked (Household, Staff sign-in, Linked phones); a chore for them (or a pet routine); a first finished task.
  • Existing households never see it: any finished chore or any routine log already on record means the home is past setup.
  • It goes away: when the first task is finished (a one-time "set up" note, only if the checklist was seen on this phone), or on Not now, remembered for this owner on this phone.
  • Today stays calm: while the checklist shows, the welcome card steps aside for a short "Nothing on this day yet" (Add Chore is in the checklist; a home with pets keeps its Add Pet button there). My tasks keeps its place below the checklist.

1. A brand-new home: the checklist on Today

Straight after naming the home. One step is open at a time, the next one to do, with its one button. Steps already done stay as a quiet green tick. Drawn for a home without pets; with pets, an Add Pet button sits under "Nothing on this day yet".
