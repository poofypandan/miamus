## 3. New, top priority before sharing with friends and family: approve new households

**The problem, found in the code:**

- `supabase/migrations/088_auth_rls_and_token_rpc.sql` has the policy
  `households_insert_own`:
  `for insert to authenticated with check (owner_auth_id = auth.uid())`.
  Any signed-in account may create a household, and that includes a staff
  phone's anonymous session if it calls the API directly.
- `src/middleware.ts` sends any signed-in, non-anonymous account with no
  membership to `/onboarding`.
- `src/app/(auth)/onboarding/page.tsx` then inserts a household and an owner
  membership on its own.
- That is how staff who tapped "sign in with Google" ended up owning empty
  households of their own. It is also how a stranger with the URL would get in.

**Proposed fix (owner to confirm the decisions below):**

1. **Platform admins:** a table of the user IDs allowed to approve. Seed it by
   hand on each project with the owner's own account. Never put an email in a
   migration or in code.
2. **Access requests:** user ID, name, email from Google, when, and status
   (pending, approved, rejected), plus who decided and when.
3. **Creation goes through one function.** A `SECURITY DEFINER`
   `create_household(p_name)`:
   - creates the household and the owner membership together, which also fixes
     the two-step create from Phase 120;
   - refuses anonymous callers and anyone without an approved request;
   - revokes `public, anon` and grants `authenticated`.

   Drop `households_insert_own`, so a direct insert is no longer possible.
4. **Onboarding.** An unapproved account files a request and sees a waiting
   screen. It is in both languages, because staff land there by mistake:
   "Staf rumah? Kamu tidak perlu akun Google — minta link undangan ke pemilik
   rumah."
5. **Approvals screen.** Visible only to platform admins: pending requests,
   with Approve and Reject. A push notification to platform admins when a
   request arrives is optional for v1.
6. **Existing owners.** Mark every current household member as approved
   (backfill in the migration).
7. **Unchanged:** co-owner invites (`/join/owner`), since the invite is the
   approval, and staff invites.
8. **Privacy policy, both languages, same PR:** access requests (name and
   email of someone not yet a user), and how long rejected ones are kept.
9. **Cleanup.** List households created by mistake (one member, no pets, no
   schedules) and show the owner. Delete only with the owner's go-ahead on
   production.

**Decisions for the owner:**

- Who approves: the owner only, or the owner and spouse?
- How to be told: a push notification, or just the screen?
- How long to keep rejected requests? Suggestion: 30 days.
- Should co-owners also be able to approve, later?

## 5. Backlog, in recommended order (after section 3)

1. **Owner/Staff separation.**
   - Owners never need the staff app. A "My tasks" card on the owner's Agenda
     appears only when an owner is assigned something.
   - In Chores, staff are listed first; an owner appears only in a week they
     have chores.
   - No database or policy change. Admins already have roster rows since
     Phase 139.
2. **Floors and rooms.**
   - Store the floor separately from the room ("LT2" + "Master Bathroom").
   - Backfill by splitting names that start with `LT<n>`. The floor is
     optional, and "Luar" (outside) is allowed.
   - Then a "By room / By chore" owner view:
     - by room: floor → room → chores, how often, next date, who;
     - by chore: e.g. Vacuum → rooms, how often, next date.
   - Plus: suggest existing chore names while typing, "add this chore to
     several rooms", and rooms with no chores or not cleaned in 2 weeks.
3. **Repairs (owner-first).**
   - Two-tap photo with an optional note and room.
   - Statuses: open, waiting on someone, fixed (with an "after" photo).
   - "Share to WhatsApp" goes through the phone's share sheet, with photo and
     text.
   - Repair people stay outside the app. No contacts stored; at most a
     free-text "who's fixing it".
   - Repair photos are kept until fixed, not deleted at 30 days, so the policy
     must change.
4. **Thank-you cards for staff.** No points, leaderboards or streaks.
   - The owner turns a finished task's photo into a card with a personal note.
     It shows full-screen once on the staff phone, then stays in "Kartu saya".
   - A private weekly recap per person.
   - Work anniversaries.
   - A nudge to the owner after a great week.
5. **Fix 3 (deferred from Phase 146):** queue saves that fail on bad Wi-Fi,
   not only when the phone says it is offline, and route routine photos
   through `lib/photo-queue.ts`.
   - One privacy-policy revision should cover repairs, cards and this, rather
     than three.
   - The policy's "kept only on the phone" list is already missing the 24-hour
     household snapshot (`lib/snapshot-cache.ts`), the offline log queue,
     chore drafts and dismissed course alerts.
