# Miamus roadmap — 9 November 2026

Everything the owner has asked for, what's built, what's planned, and what
isn't started, in priority order. Replaces the "Recommended next" list in
`docs/app-review-2026-11-07.md` (its done items are marked below). The
evening and Sunday reviews (scheduled tasks) add evidence as it comes in;
this file is the plan they're measured against.

## How it's ordered

1. **P0 — Trust and safety.** Anything that could lose data, show the wrong
   people the wrong thing, break a promise in the privacy policy, or leave us
   blind to problems. Small, so done first.
2. **P1 — The everyday problems of the households using Miamus now** (the
   owner's family, their mother's household): what they reach for daily.
3. **P2 — What a new household needs** to start, and what someone pays for.
4. **P3 — Good ideas for later**, once P1–P2 hold.

Inside each level: biggest effect on users for the least work first. Rules
that don't change: five tabs, manual first (AI is the paid extra), owner text
in English and Bahasa Indonesia, staff screens in Bahasa Indonesia, a short
tutorial with every feature that changes what people do.
