Version 5 (15 November 2026) changes one thing from version 4: Kakak's voice
is now generated by ElevenLabs on the owner's paid Creator plan. Everything
else seen and heard was made for Miamus on the owner's laptop on 14 November
2026; nothing else was bought, downloaded as stock, sampled or recorded. This
page lists each part, where it came from, and the terms that allow it in paid
social ads. The licence texts are in this folder; `elevenlabs-provenance.json`
records every line of the voice (text sent, take, ElevenLabs request id).

## Things to know

1. **Work made by an AI may not be protected by copyright** in some
   countries. That does not limit Miamus's use of the music, the pictures or
   the voice; it means Miamus may not be able to stop someone else copying
   them. The Miamus name, mark and app are protected in the ordinary way.
2. **Platforms ask for a label on realistic AI-made sound.** Kakak's voice is
   synthetic, and the film says she is an AI helper. When posting, turn on
   the "AI-generated" label where the platform offers one (TikTok requires
   it for realistic AI audio; Instagram and YouTube have the same switch).
3. **ElevenLabs: two checks for the owner.** (a) The commercial licence does
   not cover "Beta Services". Nothing in ElevenLabs' documentation calls
   `eleven_v4` a beta or preview (it is listed as a standard model on the
   pricing page), but ElevenLabs does not publish a list; confirm that the
   model picker shows no "beta", "alpha" or "preview" tag on Eleven v4.
   (b) Keep the Creator plan's November 2026 invoice with this file: it shows
   the plan was paid when the voice was generated.
4. **The voice's owner can withdraw Lyly from the Voice Library.** That does
   not affect the trailer (its audio was generated before any withdrawal).
   The 730-day notice protects only accounts that have added the voice, so if
   Lyly may become Kakak's voice in the app, add her to My Voices (Voice
   Library, "Add") now. Using her in the app is a separate decision and needs
   its own privacy-policy update.
5. **Nobody real is imitated.** Lyly is a voice shared by its owner for use
   by others; the trailer does not present her as a real person, and Kakak is
   introduced as an AI helper.
