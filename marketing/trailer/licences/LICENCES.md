# Licences: what is in the trailer, and the right to use it in paid ads

Everything seen and heard in the four cuts was made for Miamus on the owner's
laptop on 14 November 2026. Nothing was bought, downloaded as stock, sampled
or recorded. This page lists each part, where it came from, and the terms
that allow it in paid social ads. The licence texts are in this folder.

## Heard in the film

| Part | What it is | Terms |
|---|---|---|
| **Kakak's voice** | Speech generated by **Kokoro-82M** (hexgrad), run locally. One speaker, defined in `audio/voice/speak.py` as `kakak-a`: a blend of two of the model's voices for her sound, with an English melody for English and another for Indonesian. | **Apache License 2.0** for the code and the weights (`Kokoro-Apache-2.0.txt`). The model card (huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M, read 14 Nov 2026) says: "With Apache-licensed weights, Kokoro can be deployed anywhere from production environments to personal projects", and that it "was trained exclusively on permissive/non-copyrighted audio data and IPA phoneme labels". Apache 2.0 allows commercial use and puts no claim on what the model produces. |
| **The music** ("Mama, look", 60 s and 15 s) | An original lullaby in D, written as code in `audio/music/score.py` and played by instruments that are computed from sine waves and noise (`audio/music/dsp.py`). | Made for Miamus in this project. No samples, loops, sound fonts or recordings of anyone's work, so there is no third party to license it from. |
| **Sound effects and the garden's air** | Pops, taps, a shutter, chimes, wind, birds, crickets: all computed in `audio/sfx.py`. | As the music. |

## Two things to know

1. **Work made by an AI may not be protected by copyright** in some
   countries. That does not limit Miamus's use of the music, the pictures or
   the voice; it means Miamus may not be able to stop someone else copying
   them. The Miamus name, mark and app are protected in the ordinary way.
2. **Platforms ask for a label on realistic AI-made sound.** Kakak's voice is
   synthetic, and the film says she is an AI helper. When posting, turn on
   the "AI-generated" label where the platform offers one (TikTok requires
   it for realistic AI audio; Instagram and YouTube have the same switch).
