Which voice is Kakak?

Five voices say the same four lines, in Bahasa Indonesia and in English. The letters are random and the names are hidden, so you can choose by ear alone. All clips are set to the same loudness.

How to listen

  • Listen apart first, then compare your picks together.
  • Try the phone's own speaker as well as headphones: most people will hear her that way.
  • Listen for a loving older sister: calm, warm, light. Not an announcer.
  • She may also become Kakak's voice inside the app later.
