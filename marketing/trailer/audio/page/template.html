Which is which
  • Voice 2 is in the film. One speaker with two melodies: an American one for English, an Indonesian-friendly one for Bahasa Indonesia. A speech-recognition check hears her Indonesian as Indonesian and writes down every word correctly.
  • Voices 1, 4 and 5 are made the same way from other base voices. Voice 5 is the softest and slowest.
  • Voice 3 comes from a different engine. It has no Indonesian of its own (its nearest language is Malay), and the same check hears its Indonesian lines as Malay.
  • Music A is in the film: felt piano carries the tune. B puts the strings forward. C gives the tune to a thumb piano and is the lightest.
  • I cannot hear. I chose by measurement: the words, the language, the accent, the loudness. Your ears outrank all of it.

To change either one, reply with a number and a letter, for example voice 4, music B. Re-rendering all four cuts takes about fifteen minutes.

Every voice here was generated on this laptop by open models (Kokoro, Apache-2.0; Chatterbox, MIT). The music was written and synthesised for Miamus; no samples, no recordings.
